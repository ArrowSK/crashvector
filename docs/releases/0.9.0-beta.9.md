# CrashVector 0.9.0-beta.9

This corrective desktop beta stabilises articulated road users before a real collision and improves lorry aftermath framing.

- Pedestrians remain upright before real vehicle contact and then return to Godot gravity for the aftermath.
- Cyclists preserve bicycle road support while unsupported rider segments remain stable until their first real contact.
- The road-user regressions measure multi-frame pre-impact vertical stability and peak centre height during the representative collision.
- The aftermath camera accounts for the lorry's cargo-body extent, preventing a rear-origin framing error.

CrashVector remains an educational visualisation. It does not provide biomechanics, injury prediction, manufacturer crash correlation or forensic reconstruction.
