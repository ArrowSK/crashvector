# CrashVector 0.9.0-beta.7

`0.9.0-beta.7` is a corrective desktop beta for the production contact and deformation paths reviewed after beta.6.

## Release contents

- the primary passenger-car contact volume now aligns with the visible front envelope; permanent deformation waits for an authoritative non-ground Godot contact rather than probe proximity;
- confirmed rigid wall, concrete barrier and generic tank collisions retain measured impact demand, so the primary car deforms after contact without the former early visual collapse;
- pole and tree targets retain a bounded visible yielding stage at severe demand instead of appearing as undeformable fixtures at 200 km/h;
- pedestrian and bicycle targets use reciprocal physical collision masks, and their transfer paths no longer inject an artificial vertical launch;
- M17/M18 production cars retain the shared confirmed-contact bookkeeping, preserving tank, rear-impact and broadside paths;
- heavy truck, articulated truck and rigid-lorry side deformation now uses the striker's measured impact demand only after an actual target contact, restoring real-contact broadside and oblique response.

## Validation

- GitHub Actions consolidated M0–M22 production regression suite;
- parser, M12/M13 wall-impact, M14 road-user/yielding-target, M17 production/tank, M18 passenger-car side-impact, M20 heavy/lorry/motorcycle and M21 articulated-truck regressions;
- release packaging additionally validates the macOS Universal 2 DMG, Windows x64 installer, SHA-256 sidecars and generated update manifest before publication.

## Scope

CrashVector remains an educational simulation. Its generic vehicles, tank, road users and obstacles do not claim manufacturer correlation, certified reconstruction, biomechanics, injury prediction, regulatory validation or real-world crashworthiness.
