# CrashVector 0.9.0-beta.8

This corrective desktop beta makes crash presentation follow confirmed Godot rigid-body contact rather than visual probes or post-solver velocity alone.

- Passenger-car broadside targets now retain the confirmed pre-impact lateral energy needed for their bounded side-collapse and collision-face retreat.
- Passenger-car stance uses stable suspension contacts, so the displayed wheels and body remain supported by the road.
- Pedestrian, cyclist and motorcycle rider release remains gated by real contact; no probe-driven launch is used.
- The generic tank has a continuous tracked hull and compound collision envelope.
- The package workflow's full gate now includes the motorcycle-with-rider collision regression.

CrashVector remains an educational visualisation. It does not provide biomechanics, injury prediction, manufacturer crash correlation or forensic reconstruction.
