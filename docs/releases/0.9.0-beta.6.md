# CrashVector 0.9.0-beta.6

`0.9.0-beta.6` is a corrective desktop beta candidate. It retains CrashVector’s existing production simulation architecture and fixes issues found in hands-on visual review of beta.5.

## Release contents

- passenger-car wheel suspension roots now remain on the rigid-chassis road-contact plane; the selected Kenney body is mounted to those roots and each visible wheel is laterally fitted to the source wheel opening, preventing the floating-car and detached-wheel presentation defect;
- setup, live simulation and replay cameras reserve a safe margin for the centre crash viewport, so the primary vehicle and target are not hidden beneath the desktop side panels;
- the cinematic video settings panel is a full-window input-blocking modal above the desktop shell, with a scrollable settings body and a fixed action row;
- changing targets now resets all target defaults. Fixed wall/barrier targets no longer inherit a previous dynamic target’s editable mass;
- riderless motorcycles defer probe-only resistance until the rigid chassis contact occurs. A 50 km/h frontal crash now records physical contact and drives bounded visible rear/frame deformation;
- articulated pedestrian probe transfer no longer adds an artificial upward impulse, removing the direct cause of a pedestrian vaulting over the car.

## Validation

- Godot project parse validation;
- M7 cinematic export profile and production-scene modal smoke checks;
- Kenney passenger-car grounded-wheel presentation regression;
- M16.1 desktop, fixed-target and setup camera regression;
- M16.2 packaged-case aftermath camera regression;
- 50 km/h production motorcycle contact/deformation regression;
- production tank/reciprocal impact, lorry/motorcycle, articulated-truck and articulated-road-user regressions;
- release packaging will additionally validate macOS Universal 2 and Windows x64 installers, checksums and the generated update manifest before publication.

## Scope

CrashVector remains an educational simulation. The generic vehicle, tank, pedestrian and motorcycle models do not claim manufacturer correlation, certified reconstruction, biomechanics, injury prediction, regulatory validation or real-world crashworthiness.
